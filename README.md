# BHRN Connect

We are building a website for Business and Human Rights Young Professionals Network. Our proposed structure is: Home | About Us | Leadership Board | Events | Contact / Join Us 

The idea would be for Home,  About Us and Leadership Board to sit on the same landing page. The top of the homepage would give a quick overview of the Network and what we do, including programming, community/social events and resources. Clicking “About Us” in the menu/tabs would then scroll down to a section of the homepage explaining why the Network was established, what we’re looking to achieve and who the Network is for. And clicking Leadership Board would take you further down the homepage to the headshot/bio section displaying the leadership board. This way the three pages won't look too bare as separate pages.

Events would include both upcoming BHRYPN events (as well as conferences/events where we have a presence) and a simple archive of past events (to showcase how active the network has already been!).

Contact / Join Us would include our email, LinkedIn group and potentially a newsletter sign-up/contact form.

For each Board member, we’re thinking a headshot, name, job title/organisation, short bio and LinkedIn profile.

In terms of design, we suggested that it would be nice to go for something vibrant, modern and slightly more fun for a young professionals network, rather than a very traditional corporate website.
